# Practical quality gates and review rubric

Use this rubric in critique mode or for substantive drafts. Score only when the user asks for a score or a comparison. A high aesthetic score cannot override a hard evidence failure.

## Blockers (binary)

- **Grounding:** material claims trace to evidence; no fabricated quote, source, anecdote, number, or outcome.
- **Interpretation:** causal, statistical, and future-looking wording does not exceed the underlying evidence.
- **Fairness:** comparisons are relevant and appropriately defined; significant counterevidence or uncertainty is not quietly omitted.
- **Accessibility, when visuals are involved:** meaning is not conveyed exclusively by color or an inaccessible graphic.

If any blocker fails, qualify or replace the problematic statement before delivering. If it cannot be repaired without inventing facts, present a clear evidence gap.

## Diagnostic dimensions

| Dimension (0 / 1 / 2) | 0 — missing | 1 — partial | 2 — effective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audience and purpose | Unknown reader or decision | Partially tailored | Directly answers target reader's question |
| One-line message | Topic-only or contradictory | Plausible but vague | Precise, evidence-supported BLOT or open question |
| Logic and structure | Jumps / no rationale | Recoverable argument | Natural evidence-to-implication flow |
| Stakes and balance | Forced drama or no relevance | Stakes present but abstract | Specific authentic tension plus fair caveats |
| Economy and voice | Bloated, formulaic, wrong register | Mostly clear | Concise, natural, audience-appropriate |
| Action / takeaway | Unsupported ask or missing point | Generic next step | Proportionate implication, decision, or takeaway |

Total (optional) 0–12. **This is a design rubric, not a scientifically validated instrument or a prediction of audience response.** If no decision or CTA is appropriate, a meaningful takeaway earns full credit.

## Review passes

1. **Fact pass:** compare against the inputs and evidence ledger; address every blocker.
2. **Reader pass:** check the core question, clear BLOT, defensible comparison, and actual stakes.
3. **Editorial pass:** trim redundancy, preserve voice, improve headings/visuals, and remove invented urgency.

A good story can be retold in one line without contradicting the evidence. The original guide's ~3-minute version is useful for executive materials but not mandatory for short outputs.

## Evaluation discipline

For behavioral eval prompts, record **triggered?**, **evidence fidelity?**, **structure fit?**, **format/voice preserved?**, and **unnecessary output?**. Prefer relative v0.1-v0.2 comparison under the same inputs, model, and settings. The test cases in [evaluation-cases.md](evaluation-cases.md) are proposed scenarios; they are not proof of successful runtime execution.
